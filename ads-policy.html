Ads Policy

App: Letter Legends
Developer: Glitch Yard
Effective Date: September 10, 2026
Last Updated: September 10, 2026

Summary: Letter Legends shows opt-in rewarded video ads only. We do not show interstitial ads, we do not show banner ads, and no ad ever interrupts a match. You only ever see an ad because you tapped a button offering you something in return. You can turn off personalized ads at any time in Settings → Ads Policy.

1. The only ad you will see

Letter Legends is free to play, and advertising is one of the ways that stays sustainable. We have deliberately limited it to a single format:

  • Rewarded video — a short video you choose to watch in exchange for something specific: an extra wheel spin, a reward top-up, or a similar in-game bonus. The reward is always stated on the button before you tap it.

Every rewarded ad is opt-in. Nothing in the game requires you to watch one, and declining never costs you progress, currency, or access to a feature you already have.

2. What we do not do

Ad format Shown in Letter Legends?
Rewarded video (you tap to watch, you get a reward) Yes — this is the only format
Interstitial (full-screen ad between matches) No — disabled game-wide
Banner ads No
Ads during a match No
Ads before you can open the app No

We removed post-match interstitials on purpose. An ad that ambushes you the moment you finish a game is the single most common complaint about free word games, and we would rather not earn that review.

3. Who serves our ads

We use LevelPlay (powered by ironSource, a Unity Technologies company) to deliver rewarded video. LevelPlay works with a number of ad networks that bid to fill each ad slot.

Depending on your consent choices, LevelPlay and its partner networks may process:

  • Your device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) — only with your consent
  • Device and connection information (device model, OS version, country, language, network type)
  • Ad interaction data (that an ad was shown, watched to completion, or closed)

See the ironSource Privacy Policy for how LevelPlay handles this data.

4. Personalized vs. non-personalized ads

Personalized ads use your advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you. Non-personalized ads are based only on general context, such as the app you are using and your country.

You are never required to accept personalized ads. If you decline, you will still see rewarded ads and still earn the same rewards — the ads are simply less targeted.

Changing your choice

You can change your advertising preferences at any time, in the app:

  • Settings → Ads Policy — reopens your ad and analytics consent options
  • Settings → Privacy — the full privacy control panel

On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt appears before any advertising identifier is accessed. If you decline it, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can revisit this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

5. Players in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we present a consent form before any personalized advertising takes place, in line with the GDPR and the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. You may accept or decline, and you may change your answer later from Settings → Ads Policy.

6. Players in California and other US states

We do not sell personal information for money. Under the CCPA/CPRA, sharing advertising identifiers with ad networks for personalized advertising may still be treated as a "sale" or "share". You can opt out by declining personalized ads in Settings → Ads Policy, which stops that sharing.

7. Children

Letter Legends is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where a user is identified as a child, only non-personalized ads are served.

8. Removing ads entirely

Because ads in Letter Legends are opt-in, you can simply never tap one. If you would like to support the game directly, ad-removal and other purchases are available in the in-game Shop. Removing ads does not change the rest of your gameplay.

9. Reporting a bad ad

If you see an ad that is inappropriate, misleading, broken, or that failed to pay out its reward, please tell us — we pass reports upstream to the ad network and can credit a missing reward.

  • Email: support@glitchyard.studio
  • Subject line: "Ad Report – Letter Legends"
  • A screenshot and the approximate time help us identify the advertiser.

10. Changes to this policy

If we ever change our ad formats — for example, if we were to introduce a format not listed in Section 2 — we will update this page, update the "Last Updated" date, and notify you in-app before the change takes effect.
