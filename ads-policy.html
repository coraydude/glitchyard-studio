Ads Policy

App: Letter Legends
Developer: Glitch Yard
Effective Date: September 10, 2026
Last Updated: September 10, 2026

Summary: Letter Legends shows two kinds of ads: rewarded video you choose to watch for an in-game reward, and interstitials — a full-screen ad that appears at one fixed point, after the first round of roughly every third single-player game. We do not show banner ads, and no ad appears while you are mid-word. You can turn off personalized ads at any time in Settings → Ads Policy.

1. The ads you will see

Letter Legends is free to play, and advertising is part of how that stays sustainable. There are exactly two formats:

  • Rewarded video — a short video you choose to watch in exchange for something specific: an extra wheel spin, a reward top-up, or a similar in-game bonus. The reward is always stated on the button before you tap it. These are entirely opt-in; declining never costs you progress, currency, or access to anything you already have.
  • Interstitial — a full-screen ad you do not opt into. It appears at a single, fixed moment: after the first round of a single-player game, once the round's tiles have landed and before the next round begins. Close it and play continues exactly where you left off.

2. How often interstitials appear

The interstitial has one placement and a deliberate frequency cap:

  • One placement only — after round 1. Never mid-round, never at the moment you finish a game, never on launch.
  • Roughly every third game — the counter is per single-player game, so two out of three games have no interstitial at all.
  • At most once per game — once the placement is used in a game, it will not fire again in that same game.

Interstitials never appear in the tutorial, in the practice round, or on your turn in an asynchronous Battle against another player.

When you will not see them at all

  • While you hold an active premium pass, if one is available to you
  • During a grace period after a purchase
  • As a new player, during the early-game grace period

These frequency settings are configured server-side, which means we can loosen them without shipping an app update. We will not tighten them beyond what this page describes without updating this page first.

3. What we do not do

Ad format Shown in Letter Legends?
Rewarded video (you tap to watch, you get a reward) Yes — always opt-in
Interstitial (full-screen, after round 1) Yes — roughly every third single-player game
Banner ads No
Ads in the middle of a round No
Ads before you can open the app No
Ads in the tutorial or practice round No

The interstitial sits after round 1 rather than at the end of a match on purpose: an ad that fires the instant you finish a game is the most disliked pattern in free word games, and we would rather not earn that review.

4. Who serves our ads

We use LevelPlay (powered by ironSource, a Unity Technologies company) to deliver our ads. LevelPlay works with a number of ad networks that bid to fill each ad slot.

Depending on your consent choices, LevelPlay and its partner networks may process:

  • Your device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) — only with your consent
  • Device and connection information (device model, OS version, country, language, network type)
  • Ad interaction data (that an ad was shown, watched to completion, or closed)

See the ironSource Privacy Policy for how LevelPlay handles this data.

5. Personalized vs. non-personalized ads

Personalized ads use your advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you. Non-personalized ads are based only on general context, such as the app you are using and your country.

You are never required to accept personalized ads. If you decline, you will still see rewarded ads and still earn the same rewards — the ads are simply less targeted.

Changing your choice

You can change your advertising preferences at any time, in the app:

  • Settings → Ads Policy — reopens your ad and analytics consent options
  • Settings → Privacy — the full privacy control panel

On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt appears before any advertising identifier is accessed. If you decline it, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can revisit this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

6. Players in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we present a consent form before any personalized advertising takes place, in line with the GDPR and the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. You may accept or decline, and you may change your answer later from Settings → Ads Policy.

7. Players in California and other US states

We do not sell personal information for money. Under the CCPA/CPRA, sharing advertising identifiers with ad networks for personalized advertising may still be treated as a "sale" or "share". You can opt out by declining personalized ads in Settings → Ads Policy, which stops that sharing.

8. Children

Letter Legends is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where a user is identified as a child, only non-personalized ads are served.

9. Removing ads entirely

Rewarded ads you can simply never tap. Interstitials you cannot opt out of individually — for those, ad removal is available as a purchase in the in-game Shop, and holding it stops interstitials appearing. Removing ads does not change the rest of your gameplay, and rewarded videos remain available if you still want the bonuses they offer.

10. Reporting a bad ad

If you see an ad that is inappropriate, misleading, broken, or that failed to pay out its reward, please tell us — we pass reports upstream to the ad network and can credit a missing reward.

  • Email: support@glitchyard.studio
  • Subject line: "Ad Report – Letter Legends"
  • A screenshot and the approximate time help us identify the advertiser.

11. Changes to this policy

If we ever change our ad formats — for example, if we were to introduce a format not listed in Section 2 — we will update this page, update the "Last Updated" date, and notify you in-app before the change takes effect.
